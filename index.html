🔐 Stay Smarter Online

A few simple habits can prevent many common problems.

🔗

Pause Before Clicking

Check the sender, domain and context before opening an unexpected link.

🔑

Protect Passwords

Use unique, long passwords and enable multi-factor authentication where available.

💳

Never Rush Payments

Urgency is a common warning sign. Verify independently before paying.

🛡️

Protect Personal Data

Don't share OTPs, PINs or sensitive information just because a message asks.
